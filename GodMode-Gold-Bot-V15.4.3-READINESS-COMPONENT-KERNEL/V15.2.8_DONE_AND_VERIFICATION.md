# GodMode Gold Bot V15.2.8

## Deterministic Runner Intelligence

Build: `V15.2.8-DETERMINISTIC-RUNNER-INTELLIGENCE`

## Scope

This release corrects the deterministic exit and stop-management path that could bank a small profit, such as the 29 July SELL from 4015.49 to 4013.67, while the same directional move later extended much farther.

The screenshots alone do not prove the broker's exact close reason. They do prove the realised entry and exit. The source audit identified several mechanisms capable of producing that result, and each confirmed mechanism was corrected and regression-tested.

## Confirmed root causes

### 1. The trailing stop became tighter as the winner expanded

The previous formula reduced the ATR trail width as profit increased. A stronger trend therefore received less breathing room, which is the opposite of the required runner behaviour.

Correction:

- Profit expansion no longer tightens the stop automatically.
- A clean runner receives an adaptive trail of approximately 0.85 to 1.10 ATR.
- Giveback expands only when M5/M15 continuation, recent efficiency, predictor support and retracement quality remain valid.

### 2. Recovery analysis started too late

The legacy recovery path waited until price was already close to the broker stop. By then, the stop often could not be widened safely before a normal pullback touched it.

Correction:

- Runner continuation is evaluated continuously.
- A valid `BREATHE` decision can be issued before price reaches the stop.
- Widening is limited by a profitable retention floor, broker rules, cooldown and breath count.

### 3. Stale decision evidence could control a live winner

The former manager could fall back to a cached entry decision when assessing recovery. Entry evidence can be obsolete after several candles.

Correction:

- Exit intelligence rebuilds M5 and M15 context from current management candles.
- The forming market price is included in the current M5 context.
- Missing or stale context returns `SAFE_HOLD` and cannot add risk.

### 4. The legacy recovery assessor could override deterministic invalidation

When price approached the stop, the older continuation assessor could replace a defensive deterministic score with an optimistic recovery score.

Correction:

- Deterministic Runner Intelligence is authoritative when enabled.
- The legacy assessor remains only as a compatibility path when the new engine is explicitly disabled.
- An invalidated or defensive position cannot be converted into a recovery trade by stale legacy evidence.

### 5. V14 could overwrite an approved breathing stop

The deterministic engine could approve a wider profitable stop, after which the V14 state machine replaced it with the current tighter stop.

Correction:

- An approved deterministic `RUNNER + BREATHE` decision is preserved through the V14 layer.
- V14 continues to validate broker legality and all non-breathing stop actions.

### 6. Probe promotion depended too heavily on a four-second live window

A position could remain classified as a probe after it had already produced a meaningful favourable excursion because the short intent window returned to `WATCH`.

Correction:

- Promotion can now occur from verified peak R plus current context score.
- Promotion is one-way. A confirmed trade cannot fall back to probe management because a quiet tick window appears later.

### 7. Fixed giveback did not distinguish trade phases

Developing trades, confirmed trades, clean runners and invalidated trades shared much of the same stop policy.

Correction:

The deterministic lifecycle is now:

- `SAFE_HOLD`: insufficient fresh evidence, no risk increase
- `PROBE`: no widening or breathing
- `DEVELOPING`: allow the setup to prove itself
- `CONFIRMED`: moderate protection and M5 trailing
- `RUNNER`: wider adaptive trail and controlled profitable breathing
- `DEFENSIVE`: tighten or close when continuation fails

### 8. TP extension could be placed behind the live market

For a profitable SELL, the V14 TP extension could be calculated from entry and produce a TP above the current ask, which is not a valid continuation target.

Correction:

- BUY extensions remain above the current bid.
- SELL extensions remain below the current ask.
- An existing farther valid TP is preserved.

### 9. Exit attribution disappeared when a position closed

The journal retained MFE/MAE but did not preserve the final deterministic exit phase, action, score, giveback or trail width.

Correction:

Closed-trade context now stores:

- Exit phase
- Exit action
- Continuation score
- Adaptive giveback
- Adaptive trail ATR
- Exit reason codes

The live trade-management endpoint exposes the same fields.

## Deterministic decision model

The new engine scores continuation from:

- Recent M5 directional efficiency
- Recent flip count
- M5 EMA direction
- M15 alignment
- Early Intent and Early Impulse support
- Opposite predictor probability
- Current structure direction
- Higher-timeframe bias
- Winner retracement fraction
- Spread expansion
- Recent M5 structural invalidation
- Peak R and current R

It does not claim to know the future. It widens only when the current evidence supports a runner and the proposed stop remains on the profitable side of entry.

## Safety invariants

A stop may be widened only when all of the following remain true:

1. The trade is already profitable.
2. The position is classified as `RUNNER`.
3. The deterministic action is `BREATHE`.
4. Fresh M5 context is available.
5. Continuation has not been invalidated.
6. The proposed stop stays beyond entry in profit.
7. The proposed stop is legal under broker stop and freeze rules.
8. The breath limit and cooldown permit the change.
9. The position is not an unconfirmed Early Intent probe.

The engine never widens on missing context, confirmed reversal, spread failure or structure invalidation.
