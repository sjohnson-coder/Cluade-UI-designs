# GodMode Gold Bot V15.2.6 Live Predictor Recovery

Build ID: `V15.2.6-LIVE-PREDICTOR-RECOVERY`

## Scope

This release repairs the live Early Intent and Early Impulse monitoring path, places a continuously animated predictor card on the Dashboard, removes status polling from the MT5 execution path, and corrects entry-quality logic that rejected a confirmed trend-transition retest shown in the supplied evidence.

## Confirmed root causes

### 1. Predictor diagnostics competed with MT5 execution

The Settings status request could queue behind native MT5 IPC work. A slow or blocked terminal request therefore caused the predictor card to report `Backend unavailable` even when the health page and MT5 connection were live.

**Correction:** `/api/fast-sniper/status` now returns cached priority-worker state and does not call the MT5 bridge or run strategy analysis.

### 2. Priority Intent could appear healthy after a timed-out cycle

The priority loop previously updated its heartbeat after the background job returned, even when that job had timed out or become wedged.

**Correction:** completed cycles and failed cycles are handled separately. A timeout, wedge or skipped cycle changes the subsystem to `RECOVERING`, records the failure and fails the health check. Only a completed cycle advances the healthy heartbeat.

### 3. The predictor used stale market data between candle refreshes

The priority path could score a cached market snapshot while the latest MT5 quote had already moved.

**Correction:** a canonical millisecond-precision `latest_tick()` quote patches bid, ask, price, spread, tick time and the forming M5 candle before scoring.

### 4. Tick-history requests were too frequent

Repeated `copy_ticks_range` history calls increased MT5 IPC pressure.

**Correction:** tick history is backfilled at a bounded interval while each priority cycle appends the latest direct quote to a shared deduplicated buffer.

### 5. Fast Sniper quality controls used the wrong timeframe

The quality governor could assess a Fast Sniper M5 setup with the dashboard's broader timeframe candles and ATR. This distorted extension, efficiency, flip count and late-entry classification.

**Correction:** Fast Sniper, Early Intent and retest decisions now use the freshest M5 predictor cache and recompute M5 ATR for entry-quality evaluation.

### 6. The Chop Governor rejected a valid fresh trend leg

The supplied SELL retest had 84% confidence, a completed 52.5% pullback and MTF score 0.76, but was blocked because the legacy 20-bar window still contained the preceding bullish phase. It reported efficiency 0.217 and 12 flips even though the newest bearish leg was clean.

**Correction:** the governor now evaluates both the long regime and a seven-bar recent directional leg. A confirmed retest continuation can override legacy-window chop only when confidence, recent-leg efficiency, recent flips, directional displacement and MTF alignment all pass. Ordinary choppy entries remain blocked.

### 7. The live UI gave no reliable evidence of activity

The previous card showed static zero values, ambiguous `COLLECTING` text and no useful refresh acknowledgement. Duplicate polling scripts could also compete.

**Correction:** a single live diagnostics client now uses Server-Sent Events with authenticated polling fallback. It renders an animated Dashboard card and updates the Settings card with heartbeat rings, radar sweep, shimmering metric bars, scan state, sequence, tick age, loop latency, current blocker and MT5 heartbeat. Manual refresh visibly spins, reports success time, and suppresses the obsolete click handler.

## Dashboard live card

The new `Early Intent & Impulse` card appears on the main Dashboard and displays:

- Early Intent side, stage and probability
- Impulse Confirmation side, stage and probability
- Velocity, acceleration and directional persistence
- Compression release, spread quality and live-candle strength
- Current move in ATR
- Tick count, tick age and scan sequence
- Loop interval and last-cycle latency
- MT5 heartbeat
- Current qualification reason or execution blocker
- Animated live, collecting and recovering states

The card continues monitoring when no trade is open and while an existing position is being managed. Animation is visual evidence that the client is receiving status heartbeats, not proof that a setup qualifies.

## Safety invariants retained

This release does not allow Early Intent or Fast Sniper to bypass:

- market and session readiness
- stale-tick protection
- spread and spread-expansion limits
- news blackout
- liquidity-sweep and reversal protection
- stop-loss validation
- broker admission
- account and daily risk limits
- open-position limits
- validation lock and live certification

The transition-aware Chop Governor exception is narrow and applies only to a fully qualified fresh directional leg. It does not disable range protection globally.
