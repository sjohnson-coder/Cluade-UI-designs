# GodMode Gold Bot V15.2.9 Qualified Peak Runner

Build: `V15.2.9-QUALIFIED-PEAK-RUNNER`

## Scope

This release corrects the deterministic runner path exposed by a 0.01 XAUUSD trade that banked approximately 2.45 before a much larger continuation. The screenshot does not contain the broker deal reason, so it cannot by itself prove whether MT5 closed the position at TP, SL, a management command, or manually. The source audit nevertheless found two concrete mechanisms capable of producing exactly that premature result.

## Confirmed root causes

### 1. The whole 0.01 position was capped at TP4

The bot generated TP1, TP2, TP3 and TP4, but a broker-minimum 0.01 position could not be divided into four valid partial orders. The unsplittable position therefore received TP4 as a fixed broker take-profit. Once price reached approximately 4R, MT5 could close the complete runner before deterministic stop management had any opportunity to hold the exceptional continuation.

The previous TP-push path did not solve this because it pushed towards the same TP4 value.

### 2. Stop ratcheting used unconfirmed intrabar peaks

The protection floor consumed the raw maximum favourable excursion immediately. A short-lived tick or candle wick could establish a new peak and tighten the stop on the same management cycle. A routine pullback could then close the position even though the larger directional structure remained intact.

### 3. Fresh expansion had no dedicated launch lifecycle

A new breakout could move from developing to protected management too quickly. There was no explicit phase that allowed the first expansion to establish itself before peak-based stop ratcheting.

### 4. Fixed ATR trailing did not expand with exceptional volatility

A static trail suitable for ordinary M5 movement could become too narrow when the live candle range expanded to several ATR.

### 5. Production chunks referenced a missing historical main bundle

Lazy-loaded production JavaScript chunks still imported `index-V1513-JOURNAL-DRIVEN-FIXES.js`, although that asset was no longer packaged. A fresh browser session could therefore fail to load Settings or another lazy route even when a cached installation appeared functional. Every production chunk now references the packaged V15.2.9 main bundle.

## Implemented corrections

### Single-position broker TP policy

New setting: `trading.tradeManagement.singlePositionBrokerTpMode`

Modes:

- `DYNAMIC_SL_ONLY`, default: no fixed broker profit cap for an unsplittable position. The broker SL remains the hard safety and deterministic management owns the profit exit.
- `EXTENDED`: optional distant emergency TP, default 12R.
- `TP4`: explicit legacy behaviour.

The policy is applied to both automatic and Telegram-approved entries. Existing unsplittable positions with a legacy TP are detected and the obsolete TP is removed through a broker modification request when `DYNAMIC_SL_ONLY` is active.

### Raw MFE versus Qualified Peak

The engine now stores two separate values:

- `rawPeakDist` and `rawPeakR`: the true intrabar maximum, retained for analytics.
- `qualifiedPeakDist` and `qualifiedPeakR`: the peak allowed to ratchet the live stop.

A new candidate peak must remain within a configurable ATR tolerance for a configurable dwell period before it becomes qualified. A rejected wick does not tighten the protective floor.

Default controls:

- Dwell: 3 seconds
- Tolerance: 0.10 ATR
- Minimum advance: 0.05 ATR

### LAUNCH lifecycle

The deterministic phases are now:

`SAFE_HOLD → PROBE → DEVELOPING → LAUNCH → CONFIRMED → RUNNER → DEFENSIVE`

LAUNCH is available during a fresh, aligned expansion while a new peak is still unqualified. It provides:

- No peak-fraction ratchet from an unconfirmed wick
- Cost-protected break-even only after the launch threshold
- Wider initial giveback permission
- Wider ATR room
- No ordinary trailing until the launch phase ends

### Volatility-expansion trail

When the live M5 range exceeds the configured ATR expansion threshold, LAUNCH and RUNNER phases receive bounded additional trail room. The permission is capped by `absoluteMaxTrailAtr` and `absoluteMaxGiveback`.

### Authoritative deterministic management

- An approved deterministic BREATH cannot be overwritten by the older V14 observer path.
- BREATHE remains restricted to an already profitable, broker-confirmed RUNNER stop.
- A widened stop cannot cross entry or convert a protected winner back into a loss.
- Missing or stale context remains fail-closed as `SAFE_HOLD`, never speculative widening.

### Attribution and diagnostics

Open and closed trade context now retains:

- Raw peak R
- Qualified peak R
- Deterministic phase and action
- Continuation score
- Adaptive giveback
- Adaptive trail ATR
- Single-position TP policy
- Exit reason codes

This makes the next premature exit auditable from recorded state instead of inferred from a chart.

### Settings UI

Settings → `5c. AI Dynamic SL — Unified Profit Protection` now exposes:

- Single-position broker TP mode
- Emergency TP distance
- Qualified Peak controls
- Launch-phase controls
- Volatility-expansion controls
- Absolute giveback and trail caps

The same controls are included in the production Settings bundle.

## Operational limits

No deterministic engine can know with certainty that a pullback will recover or capture the hindsight maximum of a move. V15.2.9 removes the confirmed hard profit cap and prevents momentary wick peaks from prematurely tightening the stop, while preserving bounded risk and protected-profit invariants.

Live certification still requires MetaEditor compilation, exact-build Tick Guard attestation, demo forward testing, real broker spread/slippage observation, and restart/disconnection drills.
